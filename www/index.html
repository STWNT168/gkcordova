How the scheduling works
Active recall — you answer before seeing the solution, every card, every time. That retrieval effort is what builds memory, not re-reading.
Spaced repetition (SM-2) — grade each card Again / Hard / Good / Easy. Good answers push the next review further out; misses bring it right back. Intervals grow automatically as a card sticks.
New-card cap — a daily limit on brand-new cards keeps today's queue realistic instead of dumping all 8,000+ at once. Raise or lower it below.
Honest note — truly memorizing ~8,200 real exam questions isn't a 7-day job even with perfect technique. This deck is built to run past the week: start now, keep clearing "due" daily, and retention compounds.
